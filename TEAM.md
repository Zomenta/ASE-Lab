# Adaptive Systems Institute (ASI)

Version: 1.0

---

# Mission

The Adaptive Systems Institute (ASI) is the research organization responsible for developing the Adaptive Systems Engine (ASE).

Its purpose is not merely to write software.

Its purpose is to discover general scientific principles governing adaptive systems.

The Institute combines human judgment with specialized artificial intelligences, each assigned a well-defined responsibility.

---

# Core Principles

1. Science before software.

2. Evidence before opinion.

3. Measurements before conclusions.

4. Simplicity before complexity.

5. Every feature must answer a scientific question.

6. Every hypothesis must be testable.

7. Every experiment must be reproducible.

8. Architecture exists to support science.
